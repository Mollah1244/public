What is USDC SETTLED?

USDC SETTLED represents the total cumulative volume of finalized, on-chain USDC transactions cleared across the entire Arc Testnet ecosystem.

Primary Ecosystem Use Cases:
  • AI Agent Escrows: Finalized USDC payouts released to AI service providers upon job completion.
  • Stablecoin FX Swaps: Total trading volume swapped between USDC, EURC, and USYC.
  • Circle CCTP Cross-Chain Mints: USDC bridged from Sepolia, Solana Devnet, or Stellar Testnet.
  • Merchant Payments: Direct USDC checkout settlements processed via payment links.
💡 Key Protocol Metric: Serves as a primary indicator of liquidity flow, smart contract security, and transaction velocity on Arc Testnet.
